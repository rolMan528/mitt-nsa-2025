# Request for Proposal: Wireless Infrastructure Deployment

## Organization and Project Background

Organization: AE Innovations

AE Innovations is a small startup company with 15 employees working in software development and product design. The company currently operates in a basement of a house and relies on cloud services, video conferencing, and collaborative tools. We are in the early process of renting a dedicated office of a commercial building and is looking for vendors who can supply us with our wireless office network connection. The current wireless setup uses basic consumer  wireless router that cannot handle growing number of devices. This is the initial wireless router that was used from the beginning of the company. Employees experience slow speeds, dropped calls, and inconsistent coverage. AE Innovations need a reliable, secure, and scalable wireless network to support daily operations and future growth.

## Project Objectives
- Provide stable and reliable WI-Fi coverage throughout the office
- Improve network performance for cloud apps and video meetings
- Strengthen security for company data and devices
- Support 40+ simultaneous connections
- Enable centralized monitoring and simple management
- Create a separate guest network for visitors

## Scope of Work

The selected vendor will:
- Conduct a basic wireless site survey
- Recommend access point placement
- Install and configure all wireless equipment
- Integrate the wireless network with the existing switches and cabling
- Set up secure authentication and a guest network
- Test coverage, speed, and reliability
- Provide documentation and basic training
- Offer ongoing maintenance and support

## Submission and Terms

- Submission Format

Vendors must submit their proposal in a single PDF document. The submission must include all required sections, pricing details, and contact information. Supporting diagrams diagrams may be included as attachments.

- Contact Information

All proposals should include the vendors email, the main contact person and number, and address.

- Late-submission Policy

Proposals received after the stated deadline will not be considered unless prior written approval ins granted by AE Innovations. Note that late submissions will have deduction on the Vendor Experience portion of the Evaluation Criteria.

- Confidentiality

All details in this RFP and the submitted proposals should be treated as confidential. Vendors must not share internal company information, network details, or project documents with third parties without written and approved permission.

- Support Expectations

Vendors must clearly outline their support offerings, including:
  - Response times
  - Escalation procedures
  - Firmware and security update policies
  - Availability of remote and on-site support

- Change-control Process

Any changes to project scope, pricing, or timelines must be documented and approved through a formal change-control request. No work outside the agreed scope may begin without written authorization.

- Contract Duration

The selected vendor will enter into a 12-month contract, covering installation, testing, and ongoing support. Renewal options may be considered based on performance and pricing.
